A Legacy of Elegance

A Legacy
of Elegance

Founded in Nairobi by two sisters with a shared passion for craftsmanship, and timeless design, our watch house was born from the belief that luxury should feel deeply personal.

What began as a private pursuit of rare and exceptional timepieces evolved into one of Nairobi’s emerging destinations for collectors, connoisseurs, and individuals who appreciate the artistry behind fine watches.

Built on trust, discretion, and an uncompromising eye for detail, our approach blends international luxury standards with the warmth and sophistication of modern Nairobi. Whether acquiring your first luxury timepiece or adding to a growing collection, we offer an experience defined by authenticity, refinement, and personal service.

Welcome to a new chapter of African luxury watch culture — curated by women, inspired by legacy, and designed for those who value timeless excellence.
